# Measurement Safety

1. Confirm probe ground reference.
2. Verify probe attenuation and bandwidth.
3. Use short ground leads for high-speed/high-current measurements.
4. Use differential/isolated probing when common-mode voltage is high.
5. Do not let the measurement setup create shorts.
6. Confirm DMM range before current measurement.
7. Avoid earth-ground shorts through oscilloscope ground clip.
8. Check current probe degauss/offset.
9. Confirm logic analyzer threshold and common ground.
10. Validate measurement chain before trusting unexpected waveforms.
