# General Safety

1. Avoid destructive reproduction.
2. Do not repeat destructive reproduction without a changed hypothesis.
3. Do not bypass current limits.
4. Verify measurement reference before probing.
5. Preserve evidence before changing many variables.
6. Prefer observe/isolate before perturb/replace.
7. Mark every action with S0/S1/S2/S3.
8. Mark every action as reversible/partial/irreversible.
9. Do not recommend irreversible actions without prerequisite evidence.
10. If operator safety risk exists, stop and ask for safe setup confirmation.
